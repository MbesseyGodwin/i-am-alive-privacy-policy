Child Safety Standards

I Am Alive is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

Our Commitment

We do not allow any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or any harmful behavior involving minors.

Safety Measures

  • Users can report inappropriate content and behavior directly within the app.
  • Accounts violating child safety policies may be suspended or permanently banned.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement and relevant authorities when legally required.
  • We review reported content and take appropriate action where necessary.

Prohibited Content

The following content and activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation of minors
  • Child grooming or predatory behavior
  • Human trafficking involving minors
  • Any sexualized content involving children

Reporting

Users can report safety concerns through the app or by contacting us directly.
