About I Am Alive

I Am Alive is a personal safety and emergency alert application designed to help users stay connected with trusted contacts.

If a user fails to check in or triggers an emergency alert, the app automatically notifies emergency contacts with relevant information, including location details when available.

The app is designed for individuals, travelers, students, families, remote workers, and anyone seeking an extra layer of safety and reassurance.

  • Daily safety check-ins
  • Automatic missed check-in alerts
  • Emergency SOS notifications
  • Location sharing during emergencies
  • Trusted emergency contact alerts
  • Personal safety monitoring

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Because I Am Alive is a personal safety app, we must collect certain sensitive data to keep you safe. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we need it, and who we share it with.

1. Information We Collect and Why

  • Personal Information: We collect your name, email address, and phone number to create your account and identify you during emergencies.
  • Background Location Data: This app collects location data to enable location sharing with your emergency contacts during an active SOS or missed check-in, even when the app is closed or not in use. This is our core safety feature. Without background location access, your emergency contacts will not know where to send help if you cannot open the app.
  • SMS and Call Permissions: We request SMS permissions to automatically send emergency alert text messages to your trusted contacts when you trigger an SOS or miss a check-in.
  • Contact List: We access your device's contact list so you can easily select your trusted emergency contacts. We do not upload or store your entire address book.
  • Camera and Photos: We access your camera and photo library to allow you to upload KYC (Know Your Customer) identity documents.

2. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We never sell your personal information or location data. We only share data in the following ways:

  • Trusted Contacts: During an emergency SOS or missed check-in, we share your exact location and safety status with the emergency contacts you specifically selected.
  • Law Enforcement: We may share data with law enforcement if legally required to do so.
  • Service Providers: We use secure third-party services (like cloud storage for KYC documents) to operate the app. They are bound by strict confidentiality.

3. Data Security

Encryption: All personal data, location data, and sensitive documents transmitted between your device and our servers are encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard secure protocols.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Temporary Data: Location history and check-in logs are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Account Deletion: You can delete your account at any time. Upon requesting account deletion, all your personal data, location history, and KYC documents will be permanently removed from our active systems within 30 days.
